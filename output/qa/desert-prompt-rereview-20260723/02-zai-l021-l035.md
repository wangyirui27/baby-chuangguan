## Risks (non-blocking)

### L023: "How many people?" — minor clinical tone
- Beat 3-6s: Child B says "How many people?"
- "How many people?" is slightly clinical for a 6-year-old. More natural: "Wow, so many!" or "Who is in your family?"
- **Risk level**: LOW. Does not break comprehension or pedagogy.

### L025: "Do you have siblings?" — formal vocabulary
- Beat 6-9s: Child A says "Do you have siblings?"
- "Siblings" is formal/literary English unlikely in natural 6-year-old speech. Natural alternative: "Do you have a brother or sister?"
- **Risk level**: LOW-MEDIUM. A 6-year-old might not produce this word spontaneously, but it is comprehensible English.
