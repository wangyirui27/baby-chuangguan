## Required structure

Every prompt must include these exact section labels:

1. `Title:`
2. `Duration: 15 seconds`
3. `Format: 16:9 horizontal`
4. `Cinematic Style:`
5. `Source Situation:`
6. `CEFR Target:`
7. `Zero-beginner clarity rule:`
8. `Scene:`
9. `Characters:`
10. `Story Container:`
11. `Dialogue:`
12. `Action & Performance:`
13. `Camera:`
14. `Audio:`
15. `Negative Prompt:`

## Non-negotiable quality rules

- Six layers stay independent: `learningObjective`, `questionTask`, `spokenDialogue`, `visualSemantics`, `answerOption`, `videoPromptInput`.
- `Dialogue` must use authored `spokenDialogue` lines. `title` / `answerOption` must **not** autofill Dialogue.
